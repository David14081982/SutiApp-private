## Implementation and evidence

The existing synchronous local PDF generator now measures standard Helvetica text and lays out Letter pages with a wine header, reference, four financial metrics, a monetary progress bar and a four-column table. Rows remain indivisible and in their received order. The pagination planner uses the smallest feasible page count, then balances unused space while reserving the complete closing block. Column headers repeat; the requested notice appears exactly once. Footers retain a consistent position and page counts. No runtime dependency, data fetch, asset request or business calculation was introduced.

Numbering remains the previously authorized index+1 display sequence. Raw source numbers can repeat or reset and were deliberately not corrected. Monetary values retain the original two-decimal formatter; the financial summary comes from the existing entry and metrics, never a sum of schedule rows. Numeric overpayment percentages remain uncapped; only the decorative progress-bar width is bounded to its track. Missing values, preparing loans and failed-update notices remain explicit.

Independent review found an edge case with a very tall summary: the planner initially required unnecessary pages when the first quota did not fit. The final implementation allows that first page to contain only its summary and validates capacity even without quotas. Nine focused boundary checks pass, including a 25-row case reduced to the feasible three pages and explicit errors for indivisible content larger than a page. These guards change layout behavior only.
